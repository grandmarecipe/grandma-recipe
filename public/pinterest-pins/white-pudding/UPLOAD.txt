TITLE
Homemade White Pudding with Turkey and Beef Suet

DESCRIPTION
Make savory white pudding with turkey, rolled oats, finely chopped beef suet, onion and warm spices. Fill beef casings, gently poach, chill thoroughly and pan-fry golden slices for a hearty breakfast with eggs, grilled tomatoes or toast.

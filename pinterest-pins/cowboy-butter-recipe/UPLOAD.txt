Homemade Cowboy Butter Recipe with Garlic and Herbs

DESCRIPTION
Stir together creamy cowboy butter with garlic, lemon, Dijon, fresh parsley, chives and a little cayenne. This easy seasoned butter is delicious spread over warm bread or served with grilled vegetables, steak and family suppers.
